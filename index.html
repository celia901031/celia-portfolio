A note on me.

I'm Celia — a creative marketer learning by doing, currently based in London. I gravitate toward brands where the product, the story and the room it lives in are all asked to do the same heavy lifting.

PORTRAIT drop a 3:4 portrait here · approx. 1200 × 1600
01 — Practice

Marketing as composition.

I think of campaigns the way an editor thinks of a magazine: a sequence of pages, each pulling its weight, each leaving a residue. My favourite briefs are the ones that ask for clarity first and cleverness second.

Most recently I've been working across luxury retail, jewellery and emerging fashion — translating brand worlds into windows, pop-ups and the small printed things people pocket on the way out.

02 — Currently

What I'm into right now.

Reading: trade press from the 1960s. Watching: how independent designers handle their own retail moments. Thinking about: the difference between a brand that has a tone and a brand that has a temperature.
